Open Form is free. Your provider accounts have their own limits.
How much can I use for free?
Resend Free includes 3,000 emails/month and 100/day. Each inbox recipient and confirmation uses one email. Open Form defaults to 2,700/month and 90/day to leave a buffer. Limits are shared with your other projects; Cloudflare limits also apply.
How does it compare with Formspree?
Formspree offers 50 submissions/month free. Its Personal plan includes 200 submissions/month for $15/month, billed monthly. Open Form is free to self-host using your own accounts.
Can I change it or add features?
Yes. Open Form is open source. Ask your coding agent to change it or add features for your website.
How was Open Form built?
I started with a prompt, built Open Form with a coding agent, and reviewed it two or three times. It is a new tool, so test your setup and report any issues.
